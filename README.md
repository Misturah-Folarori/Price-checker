
#  Price Checker

##  What It Does
**Price Checker** is a crowdsourced price comparison platform that helps users discover and compare the current prices of everyday products across multiple stores and locations — in real time.
Whether it’s a 50g Milo at BokkuMart, Shoprite, or Jendol, users can instantly view and compare prices to choose the most affordable option. Each listed price includes the **timestamp** and **location** of the last update, ensuring price transparency and accuracy at every moment.
In short, Price Checker makes it easy for people to know where to buy smarter without physically visiting multiple stores.

##  Why It Matters
In today’s world of fluctuating product prices, buyers waste valuable time and money trying to find the best deals. Many stores adjust prices frequently, and customers have no central source to verify those changes.
Price Checker bridges that gap by providing a transparent, community-driven system where users can:
- Instantly check current market prices.
- Track how prices change over time.
- Contribute updates based on their real-world purchases.

This product empowers consumers with real-time market awareness, promotes price fairness, and encourages data-driven shopping decisions — all while saving time and transportation costs.

##  Who It’s For
Price Checker is designed for:
- **Everyday consumers** who want to save money and compare prices before buying.
- **Retail shoppers** who frequently buy household goods and groceries.
- **Small business owners** and **resellers** who need to track wholesale and retail price changes.
- **Market researchers** or **economic analysts** who study price trends and consumer behavior.

Essentially, it’s for anyone who values **transparency, accuracy,** and **smart purchasing.**

##  Key Features
1. **Price Comparison:**
 Instantly view prices of a product from multiple stores or vendors.
2. **Crowdsourced Updates:**
 Users can submit or edit product prices based on their recent purchases.
3. **Timestamps & Validation:**
 Every price update is time-stamped and location-tagged for credibility.
4. **Search & Filter Options:**
 Find products by name, category, or store with quick filters.
5. **Nearby Store Locator:**
 Integrated map view showing stores and prices within your area.
6. **Price History Tracking:**
 View how a product’s price has changed over time.
7. **User Reputation System (Future Release):**
 Build trust with contributors who frequently submit accurate price data.
 

##  Technologies 
- **Frontend:** React.js (for fast, responsive user interface)
- **Backend:** Node.js with Express.js (for API handling and price data logic)
- **Database:** MongoDB (for flexible, scalable price and user data storage)
- **APIs:** Google Maps API (for store location and distance visualization)
- **Version Control:** Git & GitHub (for collaborative development and tracking)
- **Hosting:** Vercel / Render (for deployment and live updates) 


##  Project Vision
Price Checker aims to become the **go-to platform for real-time product pricing,** driven by the community and powered by data transparency.
We envision a world where no one overpays due to lack of price knowledge. Whether it’s groceries, fuel, or electronics, users can make smarter, faster, and more informed buying decisions — all in one tap.
Long-term, the goal is to integrate **AI-powered price predictions, store analytics dashboards,** and **partnerships with major retailers,** transforming Price Checker into a full-scale **price intelligence ecosystem** that benefits both consumers and businesses.
